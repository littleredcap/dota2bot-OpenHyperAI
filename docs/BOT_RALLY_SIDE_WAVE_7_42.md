# Prepare one side wave before a grouped push

If a friendly wave is about to meet a threatening side wave on our side of the map, removing the hostile ranged or siege creep first protects the friendly wave that carries pressure forward. This does not create extra creeps, imply control of both side lanes, or justify hitting the enemy side tower alone.

The 7.42 bot only takes this job after 14:00, when at least three healthy/available allies including a position 1 or 2 are already together in the same push mode. It assigns at most one nearby position 3–5, not someone already in the main push. It needs visible enemy creeps, two available friendly lane creeps, a clear route and no visible/recent enemy contest in the main rally. Use a conservative round-trip walking plus clear estimate, no hoped-for TP: at most 8 seconds clearing and 15 seconds for the whole job. The selected bot attacks immediately rather than farming for last hits; favor siege/ranged, then rejoin behind the group on a safe route. Drop the task for active contact, base defense or route/vision loss.

Offline checks cover one assignment, stable selection, group strength, friendly wave absence, unsafe routes, siege-creep priority, and returning to cover. For client validation, opt in with `Customize.TraceRallySideWave = true` locally. Record assignments, start and return game times, and whether the cleared wave advances without the hero. At 14–25 and 40–52 minutes, separately count threatened/empty side lanes while the group is mid and whether the ready heroes still arrive at the fight. A match with no safe opening is not evidence of a malfunction or of a passing live trigger.

## Ranged-creep-only slow push

At a visible, roughly even side-lane clash near the midpoint between both standing T1s, prefer killing the single hostile ranged creep and immediately relocating to the main group. A healthy allied ranged creep and roughly even melee count/health and siege count are required. A catapult already targeting our structure, upgraded or badly uneven waves, missing tower anchors, and insufficient time or unsafe travel reject this shortcut; the previous home-side clear can still respond. The bot may hit only the original ranged target and will not switch to the next unit after a kill, deny, loss of sight, or time expiry. This small imbalance may develop into a slow push; the engine can still reset or clear it. Check arrivals of the next two waves in a client fixture, not just whether the ranged creep was killed.
