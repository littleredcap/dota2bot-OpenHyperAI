# 7.42 decision plan after the three-match loss

## Baseline and evidence

The earlier [matched three-game series](BOT_MATCH_SERIES_20261127_E2D7E93.md) used `e2d7e93`: 9:14, 7:21, 5:24. At 10:00 no tower had fallen, yet the net-worth deficits were 3,620, 4,415, and 3,970. At 20:00 all deficits exceeded 10,000. This is not a single TP fault.

1. Split **wave access** from **last-hit conversion**. At 5–10 minutes, when opposed lane creeps were in contact (five-second samples), a living core more than 1,500 from the nearest opponent creep was recorded 79/165 versus 0/142 for offlane Dragon Knights, and 66/142 versus 1/165 for safe-lane Wraith Kings. This is a distance proxy, not a count of missed last hits. In 9107461081 the home Dragon Knight fell to 344/840, returned full, then repeatedly held behind T1 with his support. In 9107476754 the home Wraith King was justifiably forced off his lane, but was full by about 4:46 and did not resume last-hitting until 6:18. The replay cannot identify the internal modes that detained him.
2. Separate **late defense** from **ineffective contact**. At 17:22 in 9107476754 nobody alive was within 1,300 of mid T1 for 12 s (393 tower damage). At 12:10 in 9107444522 there was a mid defender nearby the entire interval (330 damage, one direct defender lane last hit). A later case had four last hits but still suffered 276 damage. Position alone is insufficient; compare which units hit the tower and when those actually stopped.
3. General combat comparison uses raw offensive output in the final comparison and only approximates mana, attacks and armor. Tower fight decisions add body count and coarse DPS, with a limited ally critical-spell cooldown check. Neither is an exact exchange simulator. A short play under an active allied tower can be worth taking when a full fight is not.
4. A kill lead is not an objective plan. In the baseline, two of four favorable grouped-kill windows had subsequent tower damage; the other two were deep home-base fights. Do not treat a lack of enemy building damage after a base hold as a failed push. Check hero survival, next wave arrival, enemy return/TP exposure, and whether the next own front is covered.

## Execution order and scope

- **Side core reentry:** recover toward a visible wave on your side using a safe lateral/behind-wave endpoint, without waiting for a local 1,200-range last-hit scan or using TP. Favor closer stages with healthy cover, otherwise pull back to the next safe stage; keep active escape, delivery and tower defense protected. Next improve explicit support cover/aggro rewards and measure actual last-hit opportunities independently; do not force the support to leave every time or to take a core's reachable creep.
- **Defense:** preserve the unique timely clearer introduced by the previous patch. Prioritize enemies actually damaging the tower and siege units over a bigger but irrelevant cluster, starting with the established Shrapnel defender. Track whether cleared waves actually reduce tower loss. Broader spell-specific positioning and teamwide reservations remain follow-ups if replay evidence warrants.
- **Short fight:** use bounded contact damage, not a precise win probability. A targeted attack range persists for only one attack order when the tower already has hero aggro; recalculate each decision, do not issue pursuit. Use target mitigation and restrict affordable allied direct spells; unknown enemy spells widen the upper bound. Core exposure vetoes a trade, but a non-core can take an independently positive exchange against a valuable siege participant. This is an initial outer-tower integration, not a global replacement of the general teamfight model. Extend with known cast and cooldown observations and wider approach/reposition/disengage strategies only after measuring this bounded gate.
- **After a fight:** keep the shared coverage reservations and outward TP guard. Verify building, safe waves/other resources, and whether a proposed objective can complete before enemy returns. Only expand conversion once early income and defending improve; do not force an objective on a weak base hold.

## Three-match gate

Standing validation uses local Hard vs Hard, identical five picks on each side, FretBots/Buff disabled, same client/build and pinned runtime commit for all three games. Record each report and index entry before the next game. Compare 5/10 lane last hits and net worth, time healthy-but-off-wave and 0–10 deaths; 12–25 mid contact and first relevant clear, first 12 seconds of tower damage; trade attempts (target value, core deaths and tower retained) and 30/60-second post-fight objectives. Preserve the results of negative trials. Directional improvement in multiple games is the first gate, not a claim of a win rate. Only then repeat on another lineup, opponent, and side. No opponent-name or draft-specific branches in the policy.
